21.Q1

Why is the first-column ordering not enough to define a time axis?

Reveal criteria after attempting

Ordering is a data operation. An axis is time only if its measurement definition establishes time; the NIST predictor is temperature in kelvin.

21.Q2

Derive the centered-difference leading order for a smooth function.

Reveal criteria after attempting

Subtract Taylor expansions at ±h; even powers cancel and division by 2h leaves f′+h²f‴/6+O(h⁴), with sufficient smoothness.

21.Q3

Which part of m and k can the unit mass-spring acceleration identify?

Reveal criteria after attempting

The equation depends on k/m. Without another measurement, scaling both by the same positive factor leaves the trajectory unchanged.

21.Q4

Give a zero-residual but wrong-answer example.

Reveal criteria after attempting

For u″=0, any affine function has zero interior residual. If the intended problem has u(0)=0 and u(1)=1, u=0 has zero interior residual but the wrong right boundary.

21.Q5

What is the NIST excerpt suitable for and unsuitable for?

Reveal criteria after attempting

Suitable: exploring static response versus temperature and held-out regression. Unsuitable: measured time integration, diffusivity identification, or replication of full-data certified statistics.

21.Q6

What happens if observation noise stays fixed while difference spacing shrinks?

Reveal criteria after attempting

Noise in the numerator is divided by a smaller spacing, so derivative variance can increase even as smooth truncation bias decreases.

21.R1

What are state, residual and identifiability?

Reveal criteria after attempting

A state contains modeled predictive information; a residual measures violation of an equation; identifiability distinguishes parameter choices from observations.

21.R2

When is halving h useful?

Reveal criteria after attempting

When smooth truncation error dominates and the assumed finite-difference expansion is valid; not automatically with noisy data or roundoff.

21.R3

What quantity is missing from the copper table for an ODE trajectory?

Reveal criteria after attempting

A time-resolved state and relevant forcing/initial conditions; temperature-indexed response is not such a trajectory.

22.Q1

Why is x³ not a diffeomorphism of the usual smooth real line?

Reveal criteria after attempting

The map is smooth and bijective, but its cube-root inverse is not differentiable at zero.

22.Q2

For x0=2, can the flow of x′=x² be evaluated at t=1 along its original solution?

Reveal criteria after attempting

No. Its maximal forward solution blows up at t=0.5; the algebraic fraction beyond that is not a continuation through the singularity.

22.Q3

Write the inverse affine coupling.

Reveal criteria after attempting

Keep the first coordinate x, then use (y_new-0.5 sin x)*exp(-0.2 tanh x).

22.Q4

Derive the log determinant of the coupling.

Reveal criteria after attempting

The Jacobian is triangular with diagonal (1,exp(s(x))); log absolute determinant is s(x).

22.Q5

Does a good round trip on Iris establish a biological flow?

Reveal criteria after attempting

No. It establishes an implementation property of a constructed transformation of measured features.

22.Q6

Does an invertible nonlinear transformation preserve a Euclidean persistence diagram exactly?

Reveal criteria after attempting

Not generally. It may alter distances and therefore the scale at which simplices enter.

22.R1

What extra assumption turns local flow into all-time flow?

A suitable completeness condition; smoothness alone is insufficient.

22.R2

Why does normalization not have an ambient inverse?
