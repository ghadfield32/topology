seeds.Q1

What does one row represent, and what do its seven columns not contain?

Reveal criteria after attempting

One recorded kernel represented by extracted geometric features. The table does not include original X-ray images, acquisition dates, farm identity or an observed growth trajectory.

seeds.Q2

Why are two equal class-number differences not physical distances?

Reveal criteria after attempting

Class integers encode category identity; their numeric spacing is arbitrary. A relabeling changes the differences without changing the categories.

seeds.Q3

Calculate compactness for rectangles 2×1 and 4×1. Did their topology change?

Reveal criteria after attempting

The values are 2π/9 and 4π/25. Both filled rectangles remain disk-like; compactness is geometric, not a homeomorphism invariant.

seeds.Q4

Why must the scaler be fitted only on the declared training rows?

Reveal criteria after attempting

Otherwise parameters of the representation use held-out data. Training-only fitting defines an inductive protocol even though it does not solve missing batch information.

seeds.Q5

What space does H1 describe in the small Rips experiment?

Reveal criteria after attempting

A selected and standardized seven-feature point cloud. It is not direct evidence of a hole in a wheat kernel.

seeds.Q6

What additional data is required for a new-farm claim?

Reveal criteria after attempting

Farm/site and relevant batch/date identifiers, independently acquired new-site observations and a split matching the intended deployment, among other domain-specific requirements.

concrete_slump.Q1

Why exclude source_id, slump and flow from these predictors?

Reveal criteria after attempting

The ID is not a causal ingredient, and slump/flow are measured responses unavailable under the chosen pre-measurement task. Another task may use them only after documenting availability.

concrete_slump.Q2

For 25 calibration errors and α=0.1, which sorted value is used?

Reveal criteria after attempting

ceil(26×0.9)=24, one-based: Python index 23. No interpolated quantile is used.

concrete_slump.Q3

Why might a small calibration set return an infinite radius?

Reveal criteria after attempting

The finite-sample corrected rank can be n+1 at an extreme target coverage. A finite maximum would not implement the chosen conservative rank rule.

concrete_slump.Q4

Does 90% nominal marginal coverage guarantee this particular recipe?

Reveal criteria after attempting

No. The guarantee averages over calibration and test sampling under its assumptions. It does not give pointwise conditional coverage for every recipe.

concrete_slump.Q5

Why is a wide interval potentially unhelpful even with high coverage?

Reveal criteria after attempting

Coverage alone can be achieved by nearly uninformative sets. Width in MPa and consequences of uncertainty must be reported too.

concrete_slump.Q6

Does a random row permutation prove exchangeability?

Reveal criteria after attempting

No. It does not erase dependence, collection shifts, or missing physical batch identities. The protocol is an educational within-collection study.

leakage.Q1

How many observed kernels are there after making three copies of each row?

Reveal criteria after attempting

Still 210. The 630 software rows include constructed copies, not independent new observations.

leakage.Q2

Can row indices be disjoint while source kernels overlap?

Reveal criteria after attempting

Yes. Separate copies have different row indices but the same source ID; compare those identities explicitly.

leakage.Q3

Why can one-nearest-neighbor benefit strongly from duplicates?

Reveal criteria after attempting

An exact training copy is at distance zero from a test copy; this rewards memorization of the same evidence rather than transfer to a new kernel.

leakage.Q4

Why not repair the audit by generating unique IDs for every copy?

Reveal criteria after attempting

That changes the meaning of the unit key and conceals the problem. Unit identity must reflect the independent object under evaluation.

leakage.Q5

What remains invariant under a consistent positive column rescaling followed by training standardization?

Reveal criteria after attempting

For the same training rows, the standardized numerical arrays agree up to floating-point error. The statement does not extend to arbitrary nonlinear changes.

leakage.Q6

What group would matter for a deployment on previously unseen camera sessions?

Reveal criteria after attempting

Session identity and, as appropriate, participant/site identity and timing support. A row-only split cannot establish that independence.

claims.Q1

Compute the first symplectic Euler state and energy for the worked oscillator.

Reveal criteria after attempting

From (1,0), h=.2: p_new=-.2, q_new=.96, H_new=.4808, not .5.

claims.Q2

What does A.T J A=J establish here, and what does it not establish?

Reveal criteria after attempting

It establishes the canonical symplectic matrix condition for this linear update. It does not establish exact conservation of the chosen H for every step.

claims.Q3

Why does the sphere correction yield residual .5625?

Reveal criteria after attempting

At (2,0), the linearized correction subtracts (.75,0), leaving (1.25,0); 1.25²-1=.5625.

claims.Q4

Why is the sphere correction undefined at the origin?

Reveal criteria after attempting

The constraint Jacobian is zero and its required rank condition fails; there is no unique direction supplied by this formula.

claims.Q5

Can a direction decrease objectives with opposite gradients strictly to first order?

Reveal criteria after attempting

No. Requiring g·d>0 and -g·d>0 simultaneously is contradictory.

claims.Q6

Reveal criteria after attempting
