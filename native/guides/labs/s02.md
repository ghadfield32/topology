## Three independent challenges

1. Define the independent observation unit for ball flight adequacy and identify repeated groups.
2. Change one declared parameter in fit free and fixed-gravity parabolas and compare later same-shot points with a simple height baseline. and predict its effect before running.
3. Design a comparison that could reject the claimed benefit; explain this limitation: No validated release detector or out-of-athlete generalization; selected points may not be pure free flight.
