## Three independent challenges

1. Define the independent observation unit for sports motion and identify repeated groups.
2. Change one declared parameter in calculate joint angles and interval velocities on nonuniformly selected observed frames. and predict its effect before running.
3. Design a comparison that could reject the claimed benefit; explain this limitation: Intervals with large gaps are rejected; derived velocity is not an independent sensor measurement.
