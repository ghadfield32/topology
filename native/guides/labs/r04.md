## Three independent challenges

1. Define the independent observation unit for actual native representation training and identify repeated groups.
2. Change one declared parameter in train small 64-16-8 encoders on observed digits with agreement-only and projected-gaussian objectives, then fit frozen linear probes. and predict its effect before running.
3. Design a comparison that could reject the claimed benefit; explain this limitation: New native eight-epoch protocol, not the original Python encoder architecture; labels enter probes only, and writer IDs are unavailable.
