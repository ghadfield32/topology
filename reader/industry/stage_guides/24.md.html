Stage 24 transfer guide — Symplecticity is not exact energy conservation

Original stage learning section · Worked case · Dataset card

Start at the right level

This guide extends Symplectic integration, energy, phase and long rollouts; it does not replace the stage's definitions, proofs, exact controls or exercises. Read the beginner data primer whenever the distinction between a row, feature, group or fitted parameter is unclear. The inherited stage includes the appropriate runnable core lab.

Connect the concept carefully

The original source’s broad conservation claims remain attributed in the source audit. Our harmonic-oscillator counterexample is a mathematical calculation. Neither that calculation nor an observed SST series establishes arbitrary-horizon physical climate fidelity.

Your guided experiment

Recalculate the symplectic-Euler step from q=1,p=0,h=0.2: q_new=0.96,p_new=-0.2 gives energy0.4808, not0.5. Compare the symplectic condition with energy, phase and rollout errors separately.

Before running code, write a concrete prediction. After running, record the actual input rows, transforms and output. When using a manufactured control, label it as such. When using observed measurements, preserve units and metadata limitations from the data card.

Check understanding, not only execution

Explain why exact continuous conservation, symplectic integration and exact numerical energy preservation are different properties. Do not label an SST forecasting model a Hamiltonian climate solver.
