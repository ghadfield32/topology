Version 4 changes and preservation record

The input was the complete v3 ZIP. Its stage numbering, original lessons, primers, workbooks, code exercises, solutions, data, geometry continuation, source history, and optional-model workflow were retained. No production WMS repository or external account was changed.

Added: 21 guided sessions, 21 executed consolidation notebooks, 42 transfer questions, 84 specific outcomes, response templates, a fourth real dataset, independent numerical checks, a read-only study navigator, and source/evidence/coverage guides. The log format remains compatible with existing v3 records.

New checks exercise planar closed-pixel topology independently of boundary reduction, integer signed boundaries and small prime fields, H0 merge times through an independent spanning-tree calculation, paired prediction comparisons, and recorded-file integrity. They do not replace the unavailable external-library checks.

Test-first records include missing-function failures before the new implementation. A generated Stage 06 notebook initially contained a malformed newline in a print statement; compilation tests exposed it, it was corrected, and the notebook was executed successfully. The recorded passing suite is the final outcome, not a suppression of that failure.

The earlier handwriting comparison is not withdrawn or promoted. Version 4 adds the paired breakdown and makes its exploratory, already-exposed status explicit. The annual sunspot table is used to teach temporal availability without claiming basketball or solar event validation.

Versioned reports and old reader pages are retained for traceability. Use VERIFICATION_V4.md for the current release. Earlier verification files describe their respective builds. The complete reading volumes may repeat shared material intentionally; counts of exercises distinguish original questions from additional transfer questions and do not count card copies twice.

The release checker now transforms valid IPython magics before compilation. Browser search scripts use a scoped function to avoid redeclaration during repeated-document rendering. The actual local-navigation restriction is reported rather than bypassed or relabeled as a pass.
