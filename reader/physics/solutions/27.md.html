Stage 27 — answer criteria

Use these after an independent attempt. Equivalent valid reasoning is welcome.

27.Q1

Positive, with a sufficiently small step and appropriate smoothness. The actual finite-step loss still needs checking.

27.Q2

Each row after its sequence of projections, before summing; vanishing rows contribute zero under the declared convention.

27.Q3

Their inner products with any d are negatives of each other, so they cannot both be positive.

27.Q4

Later projections can change earlier alignments and the order matters; actual dot products disclose remaining conflict.

27.Q5

At x=0.5 the added right term is 0.1; its derivative is -0.2, so value and slope/flux matching fail despite zero interior residuals.

27.Q6

No. Axis evaluations may be reduced, but materializing all dense entries requires quadratic storage/work in equal grid sizes.

Delayed-recall criteria

27.R1. Architecture defines the function class; the loss encodes desired agreement; the optimizer changes parameters to reduce that objective.

27.R2. No. It diagnoses the specified equal-alignment system; other directional feasibility is a separate question.

27.R3. None of the full XPINN/SPINN/PIKAN benchmark suite. We ran explicit vector, interface and factorization teaching experiments.
