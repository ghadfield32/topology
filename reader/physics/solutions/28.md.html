Stage 28 — answer criteria

Use these after an independent attempt. Equivalent valid reasoning is welcome.

28.Q1

The input represents an entire field/function and the output another field/function; sampled arrays are discretizations of those objects.

28.Q2

Its multiplier is one, so the discrete mean is preserved.

28.Q3

The branch encodes fixed input sensors into coefficients/features; the trunk maps output coordinates into features; their inner product predicts the field.

28.Q4

Its multiplier is known and fixed from the differential equation. An FNO has learned spectral weights and is trained on a mapping.

28.Q5

Grid points from one function share latent information; pointwise splitting can leak the same function across evaluation roles.

28.Q6

No. It uses measured pixel intensities as inputs to a constructed mathematical operator, not measured later temperatures.

Delayed-recall criteria

28.R1. Changing input-field distribution, parameters, domain/boundary conditions, output time or observation process can change the task.

28.R2. Validation-function error, not test-function error.

28.R3. The family has a known solution operator; a neural comparison without it would hide the easiest reliable solution.
