Stage 14 — Rotations, rigid transforms, and the camera model

Route: Spatial geometry. Prerequisites: 13.

Required path — read each explanation once

Learn: Rotations, rigid transforms, and the camera model. This is the canonical explanation. Its questions identify what you should be able to reason about independently.

Predict and run: saved worked lab. Before each calculation, state the expected type, shape, units and sign of its result.

Build independently: learner notebook. Its NotImplementedError entries are deliberate assignment functions. They are not broken worked lessons.

Check after attempting: coding answers. Compare assumptions and reasoning, not only the final number.
