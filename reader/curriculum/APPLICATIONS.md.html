Apply one idea in another industry

Do not run every case before learning the foundations. Choose the observation type that matches your question, finish one baseline, then compare the topological or geometric extension. A new dataset is a new scientific context, not permission to reinterpret its units.

Domain Case / data card Important observation distinction
Healthcare Image-summary classification Feature records are not raw clinical images or a deployment validation cohort.
Food chemistry Wine cultivars Cultivar label is not a taste or quality score.
Manufacturing Plant process measurements Twenty-one days are not a large independent manufacturing benchmark.
Business Firm-year panel Firm identity and time both matter for splitting.
Environment Historical CO₂ Missing weekly slots are not measured values.
Water Annual Nile measurements A change point alone does not establish a cause.
Marine climate Sea-surface temperatures A scalar historical series is not a full atmospheric field.
Transportation Traveler choice sets Alternatives belong to the same traveler and must stay together.
Agriculture Seed geometry Feature-space topology is not a physical hole in a kernel.
Materials Concrete mixtures and uncertainty Split calibration data from fitting and evaluation data.
Handwriting Representation comparison A now-exposed demonstration test set is not a new holdout.
Stereo vision Measured reconstruction Pixels, disparity and metric depth require explicit conventions.
Solar activity Temporal analysis Later confirmation is not live detection at onset.
Material response Copper measurements A measured response is not automatically a governing differential equation.
Basketball biomechanics SPL units and motion Selected frames from one trial are not independent athlete outcomes.
Soccer profiles Profile topology Aggregate profiles are not pitch trajectories.
Modern basketball metadata ACB readiness Ten sample games are not all covered season games or tracking frames.

Open data does not mean unlimited rights or complete coverage

Read the retained data catalogue, modern sports plans, ACB data card, and optional cross-industry plans. A plan is not an acquired dataset. The exact filenames are also reachable through the reader's all-materials library.

For video, wearables, motion capture, medical or industrial systems, define the task before selecting the newest source. Preserve participant identity for grouping, but do not use identifiers as predictive features without a justified task. Respect dataset-specific noncommercial and other restrictions before using any examples in a product.

The original small hand-derived controls remain essential: they distinguish a software defect from a surprising real-data result. Synthetic and manufactured examples are labeled and never counted as observed sources.
