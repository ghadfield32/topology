A defined prediction/measurement target, independent unit, available inputs, comparison method, evaluation protocol and decision criterion.

## 30.Q2

Treat disclosed outcomes as exposed and obtain or preregister genuinely independent data/evaluation units before new confirmatory claims.

Source identities, timestamps, coordinate frame and units, observation/inference status, uncertainty, model/version and supporting evidence.

A result that fails a preregistered accuracy, uncertainty, runtime or incremental-value requirement; document it and retain the baseline rather than relabeling failure.

## 30.Q6

The actual-book text audit, independent learner assessment, measured sports/clinical/climate validation, trained VGGT runs, and paper-scale reproductions explicitly marked as further work.

## Delayed-recall criteria

**30.R2.** The source claim, its assumptions, the independent check, supported inference and remaining limitations.

**30.R3.** Repair the specific misconception and produce new evidence; do not restart unrelated completed stages or mark it passed automatically.
