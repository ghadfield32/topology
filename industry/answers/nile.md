# Conceptual answer criteria: nile

Attempt the questions in the lesson first. These criteria do not automatically assess your work.

## nile.Q1

Why is the two-mean search retrospective?

**Reasoning:** It uses observations on both sides of candidate changes within the fitted period.

## nile.Q2

How does annual volume differ from instantaneous discharge?

**Reasoning:** One is an integral amount over a year; the other is a rate at a time.

## nile.Q3

Why must the change point be selected without reading the future evaluation years?

**Reasoning:** Otherwise future outcomes influence the fitted regime description and contaminate the declared prediction task.

## nile.Q4

Which missing quantities prevent this one series from specifying a full hydrology PDE?

**Reasoning:** Spatial boundaries, forcing, inflows/outflows, storage, calibrated parameters and subannual observations are examples.

## Delayed recall: attempt after a gap

### nile.R1

What is one observation here, and when is a row not an independent example?

**Answer criteria:** One annual river-volume observation, not an instantaneous discharge-rate sample.

### nile.R2

Why is a fitted mean change not a discovered cause?

**Answer criteria:** The breakpoint minimizes a retrospective objective; other processes can explain a mean shift. Independent causal evidence and an explicit competing explanation are needed.

### nile.R3

State one unsupported conclusion and the additional evidence needed.

**Answer criteria:** 100 annual observations, 1871–1970. A detected change is not proof of its physical cause. Name an independent evaluation appropriate to the claim; explain what the existing data do not contain.
