# Conceptual answer criteria: modechoice

Attempt the questions in the lesson first. These criteria do not automatically assess your work.

## modechoice.Q1

Why do 840 records not imply 840 independent decisions?

**Reasoning:** Four alternatives describe the same decision made by each of 210 people.

## modechoice.Q2

Why exclude `choice` and the potentially post-choice `psize` from features?

**Reasoning:** The first is the target; the second is tied to the chosen mode and has questionable pre-decision availability.

## modechoice.Q3

How can probabilities sum to one and still be uncalibrated for a population?

**Reasoning:** Normalization is an algebraic property, not evidence that a selected sample represents the intended population.

## modechoice.Q4

What is analogous to traveler grouping in sports and healthcare?

**Reasoning:** Repeated shots from athletes, records from patients, and observations within sessions must be grouped according to the generalization claim.

## Delayed recall: attempt after a gap

### modechoice.R1

What is one observation here, and when is a row not an independent example?

**Answer criteria:** One alternative offered to one traveler. Four rows form one choice set.

### modechoice.R2

Why must four alternative rows travel together through a split?

**Answer criteria:** They belong to one decision maker and one choice set. Splitting rows leaks a person and can leave incomplete choice sets. The sample is choice-based, not a population market-share estimate.

### modechoice.R3

State one unsupported conclusion and the additional evidence needed.

**Answer criteria:** 840 alternatives, 210 travelers. Choice-based sampling oversamples some modes; not population market shares. Name an independent evaluation appropriate to the claim; explain what the existing data do not contain.
