# Conceptual answer criteria: grunfeld

Attempt the questions in the lesson first. These criteria do not automatically assess your work.

## grunfeld.Q1

Why is a shift within firm necessary?

**Reasoning:** It preserves entity continuity and avoids crossing from one company to the next.

## grunfeld.Q2

Why is same-year year-end value unavailable for a start-of-year forecast?

**Reasoning:** Its measurement time is after the decision boundary; a lagged record is a simplified but clearer information set.

## grunfeld.Q3

What does a known-firm future-year test not establish?

**Reasoning:** It does not establish performance on previously unseen firms or modern economic conditions.

## grunfeld.Q4

Does a capital coefficient identify the causal effect of buying equipment?

**Reasoning:** No; selection, omitted causes and simultaneous decisions are not removed by ordinary predictive regression.

### grunfeld.R2

Why lag firm value before forecasting investment?

**Answer criteria:** The supplied same-year value is measured at year end. It is not established to be available when making an earlier prediction; group by firm before lagging.
