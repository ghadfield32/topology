## Connect the concept carefully

A finite difference of annual volume divided by years has units of volume per year per change in the indexed year; be precise about what signal is differentiated. It is not automatically the river’s instantaneous flow. Real data can identify only what its sampling and measured variables constrain.

## Your guided experiment

Use known irregular timestamps to calculate difference quotients. Then list the state variables and forcing missing from the annual river table before proposing any differential equation.

Before running code, write a concrete prediction. After running, record the actual input rows, transforms and output. When using a manufactured control, label it as such. When using observed measurements, preserve units and metadata limitations from the data card.

## Check understanding, not only execution

State units on both sides of an equation and show at least two plausible mechanisms compatible with the same sparse observations. Do not hide non-identifiability behind a low residual.
