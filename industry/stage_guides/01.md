## Connect the concept carefully

A thirteen-dimensional measurement vector is not an abstract mystery: it is thirteen scalar measurements in a fixed order. A matrix collects these row vectors. Columns can be correlated, repeated or expressed on different scales. Algebraic rank is not the same as a proven physical dimension of the system.

## Your guided experiment
