## Connect the concept carefully

Reordering the rows of a point cloud does not change its set of pairwise distances. A rotation applied to all Euclidean coordinates also preserves distances. However, rotating arbitrary chemistry features mixes quantities with different meanings; it is a mathematical control, not an experimentally valid change of wine.

## Your guided experiment

## Check understanding, not only execution

Distinguish an algebraic invariance test from a scientific invariance assumption. Homotopy and homeomorphism are not interchangeable.
