## Connect the concept carefully

Taking two coordinates of a thirty-feature image summary is a projection. It is continuous as a Euclidean linear map, but it is generally not injective on the ambient space. Distinct vectors can agree in the two displayed coordinates. A pretty scatter plot therefore does not establish homeomorphism with the original feature space.
