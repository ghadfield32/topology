## Connect the concept carefully

A differentiable simulator supplies derivatives of its own computations. Those derivatives can be accurate while the model or parameters remain incompatible with a real river. Scalar annual observations may not distinguish several parameter combinations.

## Your guided experiment

Verify the manufactured damping sensitivity from Stage 29. Next write a hypothetical two-parameter water model and explain what extra observations could distinguish parameters rather than simply fit the same annual totals.

Before running code, write a concrete prediction. After running, record the actual input rows, transforms and output. When using a manufactured control, label it as such. When using observed measurements, preserve units and metadata limitations from the data card.

## Check understanding, not only execution

Check gradients numerically and check scientific assumptions independently. A differentiable calculation does not provide missing sensors, causal structure or uncertainty calibration.
