## Connect the concept carefully

The final scientific capstone should transfer the learning cycle to a new question and appropriately licensed data. The eight case notebooks are worked examples, so their displayed test partitions are already exposed. Reusing those as untouched benchmarks would undo the lesson.

## Your guided experiment

Choose one optional dataset only after reading its card and license. Define the independent unit, metric or physical model, preprocessing fit boundary, baseline and acceptance criteria before looking at the protected outcomes. Record a hypothesis that could fail.

Before running code, write a concrete prediction. After running, record the actual input rows, transforms and output. When using a manufactured control, label it as such. When using observed measurements, preserve units and metadata limitations from the data card.

## Check understanding, not only execution

Provide reproducible artifacts and explicitly distinguish reproduced source results, your new measurements, mathematical reasoning and untested proposals. Keep the actual-book audit and external physical validation visibly open.

## Evidence and continuation
