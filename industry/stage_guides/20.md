## Connect the concept carefully

The independent spatial capstone is not completed by succeeding on a medical feature table. Use this contrast to practice identifying what must change across modalities: grouping, reference standards, sensors, error units and risk.

## Your guided experiment

Compare a WDBC classifier contract with a calibrated static-scene reconstruction contract. Identify at least five nontransferable assumptions and one reusable engineering check such as hashes or split integrity.

Before running code, write a concrete prediction. After running, record the actual input rows, transforms and output. When using a manufactured control, label it as such. When using observed measurements, preserve units and metadata limitations from the data card.

## Check understanding, not only execution

Your report distinguishes reusable method from domain validation. No medical, sports, robotic or industrial deployment is certified by a course notebook.
