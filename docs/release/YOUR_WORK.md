# Your work belongs here

This directory intentionally contains no completed learner submissions. Save your own stage answers, proof attempts, edited notebooks or short reports. Use distinct filenames for independent assessments and delayed recall so the evidence remains interpretable.

Suggested format:

```text
Stage and exercise IDs:
Date:
What I attempted before opening hints:
My reasoning or implementation:
Observed outputs and exact command:
What the result supports:
What it does not support:
Errors corrected and help used:
Next exercise:
```

Evidence paths for the learning CLI must be actual files inside the course directory. Keep an external backup before updating the course. Core tests do not require your learner notebooks to remain unfinished.


In v10, blank answer templates are tracked under `curriculum/response_templates/`; personal answers remain under `my_work/` and are ignored by Git.
