## Depth levels

Foundational definitions and elementary proofs are taught and worked locally. The main finite F₂ computations are implemented and tested. Major theorems receive precise statements, assumptions and proof strategies plus directed free readings. Full proofs of surface classification, π₁(S¹), general nerve results and interval/stability theorems are not all reprinted as complete textbook-length treatments. See DEEPENING_PROOFS.md for the required learning evidence at each level.

Rips and pixel persistence, finite-diagram matching, landscape/image representations, Mapper graph construction, and the model comparison execute in the core. General alpha construction and independent Ripser/GUDHI comparisons require the optional packages and are explicitly unexecuted here. The analytic Čech triangle exercise is not a general Čech implementation.

There is no real sports dataset or production WMS integration in this archive. The transfer protocol is a proposed independent capstone. Advanced subjects are identified, not silently claimed mastered.

Use `progress/book_audit.json` to record actual-book sections when available. No topic is marked covered merely because the title sounds familiar.
