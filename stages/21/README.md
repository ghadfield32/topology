# Stage 21 — Rates, equations, units and what data can identify

**Prerequisites:** Stages 01, 10 and the rates primer. Review matrices and the chain rule; no prior mechanics is assumed.

**Data interpretation:** Observed NIST copper measurements; manufactured cosine derivative checks. Temperature is not a time axis.

Check [conceptual answers](../../physics/solutions/21.md) after attempting the questions.

Each stage has six conceptual questions, three coding exercises and three delayed-recall prompts. Save your work under `my_work/21/`; no successful reference run grades your answers. Record one explanation of what the experiment does not establish.
