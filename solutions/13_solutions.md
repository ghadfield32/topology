# Stage 13 conceptual solutions

Attempt the lesson questions first. These are answer criteria, not scores assigned to your work.

## 1. Define a chart, an atlas, and the domain of a transition map.

A chart is a homeomorphism from an open manifold region to an open Euclidean region; charts cover the manifold to form an atlas. The transition ψ∘φ⁻¹ is restricted to φ(U∩V).

## 2. Compute the two chart coordinates of (0.6,0,0.8) and invert them.

The coordinates are (3,0) and (1/3,0); substitution into their respective inverses returns (0.6,0,0.8).

## 3. Prove that the inverse stereographic formula lies on the unit sphere.

With q=u²+v², the squared numerator norm is 4q+(q-1)²=(q+1)², exactly the squared denominator.

## 4. Derive the equation pᵀv=0 for tangent vectors.

Differentiate the constraint p(t)ᵀp(t)=1 to obtain 2pᵀp′=0. Conversely the trigonometric curve in the lesson realizes every tangent v.

## 5. Why does normalizing a real point cloud to unit vectors discard depth?

Every positive multiple λp becomes the same normalized direction. Radial length cannot be recovered from the normalized vector alone.

## 6. Explain why perspective projection is not generally a chart of 3D space.

Different positive points on one viewing ray produce the same pixel. A chart must be one-to-one on its declared domain, and a full 3D scene does not satisfy this condition.
